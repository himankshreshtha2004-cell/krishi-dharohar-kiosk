How the kiosk behaves

1Attract screen. Title, glowing India map, drifting seeds and a pulsing scanner orb ask visitors to place a crop card.
2Card scanned. A wipe transition opens that crop: map highlights the top producing states (numbered), legend cards show each state's share, then production figure and 5-year trend bars animate in.
3Another card. Placing a different card switches straight to that crop.
4Idle. The thin bar at the bottom counts down 45 s; with no new card the kiosk returns to the attract screen.

Keyboard: 1–5 scan a crop, H or Esc home, R replay. The exported files contained the screen designs only; the scan and idle logic here is a simulation of how the real app is meant to behave.
